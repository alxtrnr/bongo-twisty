### Cycling
* Surly Long Haul Trucker Disc
* Pinnacle Dolomite
* Hammerhead Karoo bike computer

The Surly is a reliable, heavy workhorse. Comfortable but slow. The Pinnacle is a bit lighter and probably better suited to Audax rides but it's not quite as comfy to ride. Both have a nine speed chainset for durability. A triple on the Surly, compact double on the Pinnacle. Both have TRP Spire mechanical disc brakes. Powerful enough for me and easy to service and/or repair.

The Karoo is perfect for me. Maps and navigation are excellent. More display field options than I shall ever need. Superb battery life. Highly recommended.

### Computing
* Entroware Proteus
* Dell XPS 13
* Lenovo IdeaPad Flex 15D (Navidrome server)
* Debian 13 (stable)
* Ubuntu Server 24.04 LTS (Navidrome)

The Entroware and Dell are more than good enough for what I use them for (aside from the sound card for playing music, hence the Focusrite interface and JBL monitors). The Lenovo was lying around doing nothing so I put it to good use. It's old and low spec but good enough to use as a Navidrome server. Ubuntu on my laptops for over ten years until switching to Debian early 2026. Ubuntu created a smooth and easy exit from Windows. It was a good introduction to Linux. We've both changed. The time felt right to go.
