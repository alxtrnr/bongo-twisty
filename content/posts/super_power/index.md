This post is my response to the Blaugust2026 group project for August 10th. I may be a day late but with the application of my chosen super power I don't think that matters much. 

![](cover.webp)

Just over a week ago I finished reading the [Gone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gone_(novel_series)) series of books by Michael Grant. The main characters in the stories develop powers. These included light-blasting hands, telekinesis, gravity cancellation, incredible strength, healing, and super speed. Each power had benefits but also magnified a flaw, exposed a vulnerability, or was connected to a malevolent entity. There was a drawback or cost to each respective character for the power they gained.

Thinking about the group project question the challenge was in coming up with a superpower that did not carry that kind of cost. Something that wouldn't corrupt, be weaponised, turned inward or damage me for having it. 

Initially I considered the power to end all suffering. I could do better than that though. Adopting a deficit based superpower would be a missed opportunity and unbecoming of someone who works in health and social care. Besides you could argue that suffering is necessary to feel fully alive. Eradicating all suffering risks cancelling out other positive experiences, emotions and feelings.  I wouldn't want to strip those things away.  Life without discomfort might become stagnant, stunted and one dimensional. No heads without tails. Yin and yang. 

How about controlling the climate? Not thinking big enough with that. It's also another deficit based superpower. A power to fix something that is wrong. To compensate for something that's missing. 

I have instead settled on the ability to make it so that every living creature on Earth has sustainable living conditions. Food, water, shelter, clean air, viable habitat. All of it, permanently, simply by willing it so. A strengths based approach. 

I'd only have to use it once and then it would be done. No human, animal or plant would ever die of thirst or hunger or exposure again. The climate would be ideal. Ecosystems would thrive in symbiosis.

There would be an end to suffering that serves no purpose and comes about simply because conditions are inadequate. Get that sorted and a lot of preventable misery would disappear while what makes life meaningful remains

There would be just one act with global effect. No ongoing  management, governance or other such burdens. I would just make it so.

Failing that I put my faith in Alan and Lucy[^1]...
