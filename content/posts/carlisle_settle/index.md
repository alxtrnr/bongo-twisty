While up in the Lake District on holiday spouse and I took a ride on the [Settle–Carlisle line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Settle%E2%80%93Carlisle_line). It's "*one of the world’s most scenic railway journeys*" and runs across regions of the Yorkshire Dales and the North Pennines. 

We travelled on one of the regular passenger trains along the route. We got a table seat so were both by the window. We looked out for them but neither Bill Nighy nor Michael Portillo were anywhere to be seen. 

The weather was good - blue skies, fluffy clouds and clear. As the windows cannot be opened picture taking was a case of holding the phone to the window and snapping away at the passing scenary.
