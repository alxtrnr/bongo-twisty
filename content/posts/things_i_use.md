I read today a piece about digital cameras and wired headphones making a bit of a comeback. It's not news. I've read about this a few times over the past few years. What comes around and all that. However, it struck a chord with me since some of this stuff I have never fully moved away from or have still got tucked away in a draw somewhere until I am minded to have a play or finally get rid of it in one way or another. I was thinking about taking a few pics and writing a post about some of this stuff. Though better of it and decided to do one of those "Things I Use" I've seen on other people's blogs. Probably better as a page for updating. I'll get round to that another time. 

Some of this stuff I am very happy with. Some of it could be better and will be swapped out if and when I find a good alternative. 

### Cycling
* Surly LH Disc Trucker
* Pinacle Dolomite
* Hammerhead Karoo Bike Computer

The Surly is a reliable, heavy workhorse. Comfortable but slow. The Pinacle is a bit lighter and probably better suited to Audax rides but it's not quite as comfy to ride. Both have a nine speed chain set for durability. A triple on the surly. Compact double on the Pinacle. Both have TRP Spire Mechanical disk brakes. Powerful enough for me and easy to service and/or repair. 

The Karoo is perfect for me. Maps and navigation are excellent. More display field options than I shall ever need. Superb battery life. Highly recommended.
